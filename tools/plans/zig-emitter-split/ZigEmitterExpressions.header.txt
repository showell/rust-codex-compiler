 EVERY EXPRESSION SHAPE, AND THE ANSWER FOR EACH. `emit-zig-expr` is the switch
 over the IR's expression nodes, and everything else here is one arm of it or
 something an arm needs: names and how they are renamed, the builtin table,
 binary operators, list and record literals, match in both its switch and its
 else-chain form, and act blocks.

 The split follows csharp's, which took the same seam: CSharpEmitter.codex and
 CSharpEmitterExpressions.codex are one chapter across two files, and the
 expressions are the half that grew.

 WHAT IS NOT HERE. Application and closures are on page 3, because zig has no
 function values and the struct that stands in for one is a subject of its own.
 Definitions, types and chapter emission stay on page 1.

 AN EMPTY LIST CANNOT ANSWER FOR ITSELF, which is why `ZigCtx.expected` exists
 and why every construct that knows the type sets it for its children. Read it
 in one place -- `emit-zig-list` -- and set it wherever a type is known.

 A NAME IS LOOKED UP THROUGH THE RENAME TABLE FIRST, before builtins and before
 constructors, because zig forbids shadowing and Codex does not. `zig-renamed-scan`
 scans from the END so the binding in scope now is the one that answers.
